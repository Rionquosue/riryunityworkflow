## Introduction

Hello all readers, I would like to open that ALL text in this document is human-written, not machine assisted, strictly on the basis of morals.

Why? I feel like having the clanker tell you why I'm using a clanker would be utterly pointless. 

I will open with just saying, the purpose of using anything that marketing material would like to call an "AI" should always be, making your life better. Not doing creative tasks for you, not commodifying culture and certainly not taking creative agency away from you, a person.

This manifesto document will bring you up to speed with my personal viewpoints, what restrictions I have had on the creation of any tools, and why I decided that it was morally acceptable to do this in the first place. 

## Core Principles

I believe creativity is the domain of humans. I refer to LLM's as "clankers" with a level of derogatory intention as I do not see any of these as AI. They are facimiles of intelligence, often anthropomorphized by marketing material, but they are not intelligent themselves.

All LLM's function on what is in essence, an extremely large vector database that provides a probability table of what words will follow your opening sentence. If you set them up to say "I will destroy the earth" that is what they will say. They are not smart, they are not intelligent, at best they are overproductive interns.

What are they good at?
Ingesting huge volumes of data and spitting out a collapsed context with I would generously say 80-90% accuracy.
Generating large amounts of safe, boilerplate data. They do not often create code that would cause memory leaks, buffer overflows, etc.
Raw brute force solutions or reverse engineering (monkey typewriter type shit.)

What are they bad at?
Generating a codebase that is safe, and secure.
Ingenuity (they do not come up with new solutions.)
Intuition (They cannot tell what people need.)
UI (everything is just the same framework. They suck at this.)


All clanker code adheres to the following restrictions:
1. Does not provide anything more than a tool.
2. Strictly permits the user to have creative agency over the project.
3. Is created strictly for the purpose of accelerating an existing workflow, or reducing repetitive tasks.
4. No clankers are to execute on the outcome of this code. It is all standalone.

## Ethical Considerations

All AI/LLM/Clanker generated code is essentially an abstraction from what it was trained on. This abstraction includes random noise and many, many layers of corrections. But at its core, AI generated code is still code plagiarized and put through so many filters that it's unrecognizable.

The machine does not inherently comprehend how the code runs. However the machine, as a pattern recognition engine, is very good at recreating patterns. The vast majority of code is patterns.

As a result of this, I do not think it socially acceptable to publish a product, page, supplement, asset, etc that is made with clanker assistance or code as anything short of entirely free and open.

### Creative agency, and why using a clanker-made tool does not detract from it

At its core, humanity, culture, subcultures, fandoms, etc thrive only on creativity. The machine is not creative. The machine does not care. Restriction breeds ingenuity, and I believe that itself is beautiful in its own way. I believe if you want a fully AI-Free life, you deserve that for yourself.



### You Deserve to Know

All AI generated things should be disclosed. People deserve more control over the direction of their lives than what this world likes to foist upon us, if you morally object to something, you deserve to know that it's included in XYZ thing. Much like the blood diamond trade, Acai berries being stripped from the people that ate them, or any other moral dillema that has to do with products.

I do not believe these tools are inevitable. They sure as fuck aren't profitable. Nor do I ever see them being, they have innovated themselves out of relevance and I believe long term, datacenters are too vulnerable, and resource hungry to work out. They are not the ideal solution nor final form. Every year we see more distillation and creative ways to make more capable agents run on smaller amounts of hardware, a big chunk of this code was also assisted by a small model that runs on 12gb of vram on a 3060. Long term, if you opt to have a clanker in your life, the only efficient way to have one is by running it locally.

## Why do I think this is Acceptable?

One of the arguments people have made since the start of this 'revolution'(it's not) is that they want the AI to do their dishes instead of making music art and expression on their behalf.

This is my attempt at doing this for myself. This essentially automates away what I consider "chores" in the unity editor. It means I have to use my ergonomic mouse less, and that even after working 45+ hours a week at a trash job, I can still sit down and have the time to actually execute on my creative visions or update my products.

I have set strict limits on what these tools can actually do for that purpose. The vast majority of what this started with was strictly tools to prevent corruption, crashes, or instability destroying my work. (GUID errors from a power outage or force shutdown for example.), other tools are created strictly to avoid having to repetitively do a task. An example is "Constraint trains", something I use on almost all of my personal models. For about 10 bones, this requires 2-3 hours of manual setup in the base unity editor.

Fine tuning and how it's actually assembled is where the creative vision comes in, much like you can either follow the instructions or take lego sets apart to create something new.
